Local-first by design

Your machine. Your choices.

Resume files and app data stay in your local ATSCheck folder. Provider keys use your operating system's credential store. Cloud AI providers receive only the content needed for the action you request; use a local Ollama model if you prefer local inference.

You bring the provider account, keys, and model choices. ATSCheck doesn't include paid AI access.
