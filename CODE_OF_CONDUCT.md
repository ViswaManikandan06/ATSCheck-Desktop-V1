# Community Code of Conduct

We are committed to making participation in ATSCheck Desktop welcoming and
respectful for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Discuss ideas and code rather than attacking people.
- Accept feedback graciously and help keep discussions focused.
- Respect privacy: do not publish another person's resume, credentials, or
  personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, deliberate
publication of private information, and disruptive or bad-faith conduct are not
acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the
repository's security contact or private reporting channel. Do not post private
personal information in a public issue. Maintainers will review reports
promptly and may remove content or restrict participation when needed.

This code of conduct applies in project spaces and when someone is representing
the project in public.
